## India payment, fulfillment, inventory, and invoicing fit

### Takeaway
The important India-specific integrations could not be verified in this run. Treat the availability and quality of payment, UPI, COD, courier, inventory, and GST invoice workflows as open diligence items rather than assumed WooCommerce strengths.

### Cited Findings
- No official integration documentation was retrieved because Agent Reach returned `RATE_LIMITED` on its first web query and the channel was stopped as instructed.

### Inferences
- For a limited-stock launch, the platform should prevent overselling and make stock changes easy to handle across the storefront and any sales channels the owner actually uses. Confirm the complete flow, including cancellations, returns, exchanges, and failed COD delivery, before selecting an integration.
- Before choosing a gateway or courier integration, compare current India eligibility, fees, settlement timing, UPI and COD support, serviceable pincodes, tracking, support, refunds, and plugin update history against the actual fulfilment workflow.
- Ask an India-qualified accountant which GST registration, tax invoice, product disclosure, and packaged-commodity obligations apply to this seller and product setup. These requirements were not researched or legally assessed here.

- Shiprocket or other courier plugin capabilities, rates, COD remittance, coverage, and returns workflows were not verified.
- Native WooCommerce stock handling and current India GST invoice extensions were not verified.

## SEO flexibility and a lean catalog strategy

### Takeaway
No current platform SEO comparison was completed because the required Agent Reach research channel was rate-limited. For the stated launch constraints, a small catalog with only genuinely useful, in-stock collection pages is a sound planning inference regardless of platform; do not treat that as evidence that one platform ranks better.

### Cited Findings
- No official WooCommerce, WordPress, Shopify, or Google documentation was retrieved in this research run; the Agent Reach channel failure prevented source verification.

### Inferences
- Launch SEO around the real products and their distinctive, truthful attributes. Keep pages focused on available inventory, and add categories only when stock and customer demand justify a useful landing page.
- Platform SEO choice should be evaluated on the actual store’s ability to publish crawlable product/category content, control URLs and metadata, manage structured product data, generate sitemaps, and avoid indexing thin filter/search URLs. A list of “SEO features” alone cannot establish organic performance.
- India-first should shape shipping, prices, currency, support, and policy content at launch. International localization and country-specific URL structures can wait until expansion is a concrete decision.

### Gaps
- Official WooCommerce/WordPress and hosted-platform SEO documentation was not checked.
- No current search results, keyword demand, competitor evidence, or platform-specific performance data was collected.
- It remains unknown which product types, materials, styles, price points, stock quantities, and restocking cadence will define the initial range.
