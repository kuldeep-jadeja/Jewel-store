# Choose a Lean Platform Before Scaling

For an India-first jewellery launch with limited stock, **my provisional default is Shopify if you want the simplest managed launch; choose WooCommerce if you already know WordPress or have dependable technical help and value more control**. That recommendation is about operating fit, not SEO superiority. The research pass could not verify current platform pricing, Indian payment or courier integrations, or official SEO capabilities because Agent Reach web search was rate-limited, so this is a decision framework rather than a completed vendor comparison. Keep the first build small and test the full order, stock, delivery, cancellation, and refund flow before committing to apps or custom development. For search visibility, useful and accurate product pages and sound technical setup matter more than a platform's “SEO-friendly” label.

## A managed launch reduces technical work; WooCommerce needs an owner

My practical assessment is to favor a hosted platform when your priority is getting a small catalog online with less infrastructure to manage. WooCommerce is a sensible alternative when you or a support person can take responsibility for hosting, updates, backups, security, and extension compatibility. Those are trade-offs to validate against your own skills and support; this research did not verify current platform terms or compare total costs. Limited stock alone does not decide the platform: what matters is reliable quantity control and simple updates when an item sells, is cancelled, returned, or sold through another channel.

## SEO should not decide the platform by reputation

No evidence in this research establishes that Shopify or WooCommerce ranks better for this store. Compare the actual candidate setups for crawlable product and collection pages, editable page titles and descriptions, sensible URLs, product structured data, sitemaps, mobile performance, and control over thin filter or search URLs. Start with pages for real products and cohesive groups that are available to ship; avoid filling the site with empty categories or near-duplicate pages. Google Merchant Center can add product visibility, but it does not guarantee exposure; the launch plan notes that India listings need shipping settings or shipping costs represented in product data ([Google Merchant Center: Free listings](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13889434?hl=en)).

## Verify India checkout and real total cost before choosing

For each finalist, confirm current Indian eligibility and fees for the payment methods you intend to offer, COD controls, courier coverage by PIN code, tracking and returns, stock behavior, invoice/accounting workflow, customer support, and export options. Compare the full cost of subscription or hosting, themes, apps or extensions, payment charges, and ongoing maintenance; no current figures were verified here. Run a test purchase through payment or COD, stock deduction, dispatch, tracking, cancellation, and refund using your actual catalog and delivery area. Also settle your launch budget, SKU/variant count, technical comfort, and whether Instagram, WhatsApp, or marketplaces will share inventory.

## Conclusion

Start by evaluating Shopify as the low-maintenance default and WooCommerce as the control-oriented alternative. Make the final choice only after both pass the same India-specific checkout and stock test and their current total costs fit your budget. Treat SEO as a site and product-quality discipline, not a reason to pay extra for a platform before demand is proven.
